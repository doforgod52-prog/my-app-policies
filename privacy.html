Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CTG handles information when users connect their social media accounts to our social media automation workflow.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how CTG handles information when users connect their social media accounts to our social media automation workflow.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, the application may process:

  • Social media account information made available through authorized platform APIs.
  • Authentication tokens needed to connect authorized accounts.
  • Posts, captions, videos, images, and scheduled publishing details supplied by users.
  • Technical logs needed to troubleshoot errors and maintain the workflow.

3. How We Use Information

We use information only as needed to operate the workflow, connect authorized social media accounts, publish or schedule content at the user's request, and troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be transmitted to the social media platforms that users choose to connect and to service providers required to operate the workflow. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information and authentication credentials. Access to credentials should be restricted to authorized people. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the stated purposes or applicable legal requirements. Users may contact us to request deletion of their information. They can also revoke app access through the relevant social media platform where supported.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party services, including TikTok and other social media platforms. Their own privacy policies and terms also apply to their handling of information.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
